Delete your Ludo Royale account

Ludo Royale · Sun Game Studio · app com.ludoroyale.app

You can delete your Ludo Royale account and the data attached to it at any time, either inside the app or by writing to us. You do not need the app installed to ask us.

In the app

  1. Open Ludo Royale and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. If you play again later, you start with a new account.

By e-mail, if you no longer have the app

Write to support@sungamestudio.com with the subject Ludo Royale account deletion.

Tell us your friend code (shown on your Profile, starting with u_) and your display name. The app never asks for an e-mail address or phone number, so these are how we find your account. If we cannot be sure which account is yours, we write back before deleting anything.

We answer and complete deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is not

DeletedNot affected
Your account and its sign-in links (Google, Facebook or Apple), the link to your device, and your sessions, so you are signed out everywhere
Your display name
Your coins and diamonds, including any you bought
Your purchase records (coin packs you bought)
Your XP, level, missions and rewards
Your collection and equipped items
Your friends and friend requests
Reports you filed, and reports filed about you		 Statistics that do not identify you: counts of active players, which use a one-way code instead of your account number and expire within 40 days, and totals of app errors
Usage statistics in Google Analytics, if you allowed them; they are not linked to your account and Google keeps them for at most 14 months
Your Google, Facebook or Apple account itself: to remove Ludo Royale's access there, use that account's settings

Short-lived game data held in our servers' memory, such as other players' recent-players lists and the weekly ranking, expires on its own within 21 days.

Deletion is permanent. Coins, diamonds, progress, items and friends cannot be restored or moved to another account. Deleting your account does not cancel or refund a purchase; refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple.

What does not delete your account

Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account, because it is kept on our servers. If you sign out of a guest account without saving your progress, you can no longer reach it, but it still exists until you delete it as described above.

More about what we collect and why is in the Ludo Royale Privacy Policy. Looking for King Teen Patti? See King Teen Patti account deletion.
